# TNT Tag



***

## What is it about?

TNT Tag is a classic of classics if you don't know what it is you live under a rock hahaha

But I'm here to explain what it is

You have an item that you select, that item, whoever has it will have 60 seconds (or whatever you define in the config) to hit someone else and give them the item, if they do not pass it in the established time, they lose and another one is chosen until the game time ends or only one person is left standing

***

It is the Classic TNT Tag where you must survive the one who got the TNT and not be exploited

If you haven't played it, I recommend it

You can configure it via: `/ge configure tnttag` or by: `tntTag.yml`
