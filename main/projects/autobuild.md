» Build smarter, not harder.\
Auto Build lets you create, save, and deploy automatic structures directly in-game — no WorldEdit, no `.schem` files, no headaches.\

Players simply place a special configurable block, and the structure instantly appears — perfect for automation, rewards, or rank perks.\
