## **Installation**

Buy the plugin from [BuiltByBit](https://builtbybit.com/resources/game.58874/)

When you buy the plugin click on the download button

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Drag and drop the .jar and dependencies into your server's plugins folder![](<../.gitbook/assets/Game Event.gif>)
